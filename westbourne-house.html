About Westbourne House

Westbourne House offers secure and affordable accommodation for students in Gqeberha. The property includes Wi-Fi, study areas, transport access, and modern rooms — everything you need to focus on your studies.

  • Address: Westbourne Road, Port Elizabeth Central, Gqeberha, South Africa
  • Province: Eastern Cape
  • City: Gqeberha
  • Gender: Male & Female Students
  • NSFAS Accredited
  • Study-Friendly Environment
  • Price: R3 200 /month
