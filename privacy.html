Your information matters

Privacy Policy.

We keep the information you share with us focused on one purpose: helping you enquire, book and communicate with Sir Kabs Driving School.

Plain-language summary

We do not sell your personal information. We use the details you submit to respond to enquiries, manage booking requests and provide driving-school services. This policy is intended to support responsible processing under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

01

Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies when you use the Sir Kabs Driving School website, submit the booking form, contact us by email or phone, or choose to contact us through WhatsApp.

02

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive your full name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, requested service, preferred date and time, driving experience, learner's licence status, pickup or meeting area, custom package details and any message you choose to send.

Please do not send identity numbers, medical details, financial information or other sensitive information unless it is genuinely needed and we have specifically asked for it.

03

How we use your information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, process and confirm booking requests, contact you about the service you requested, arrange lessons or test-day support, keep reasonable business records, deal with service issues, and protect the website and business from misuse or fraud.

We aim to use information only for purposes that are relevant to the reason it was collected, or where another lawful reason allows the processing.

04

When information may be shared

Booking-form information is sent to the Sir Kabs business email through FormSubmit. The website is also prepared to send booking information to a Make.com webhook when that integration is configured. Our website host and email provider may process limited information as part of providing their services.

If you choose the WhatsApp buttons, your communication is handled through WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information where required by law, a lawful authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety or property.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

05

Cookies and technical information

The current Sir Kabs website code does not use advertising or analytics cookies. A hosting provider may still receive ordinary technical information such as an IP address, browser details and server logs when a website is visited. If analytics, advertising or additional cookie-based services are added later, this policy should be updated and any consent mechanism required by law should be added.

06

Retention and security

We keep booking and contact information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, for legitimate business administration, or where a legal obligation requires retention.

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure. No website, email service or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you, request access to it, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, or request deletion or restriction where appropriate. You may also object to certain processing where the law gives you that right.

To make a privacy request, email mwmakabane@gmail.com or call +27 78 086 1628. We may need enough information to verify that the request relates to you.

Information Regulator (South Africa)

If you believe your personal information has not been handled properly, you may also contact or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator. Visit inforegulator.org.za or email enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

08

Younger learners

Some learner drivers may be under 18. Where appropriate, a parent or guardian should assist with a booking and with the sharing of personal information. Please provide only the information needed for the requested service.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, booking process, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a material change is made.
