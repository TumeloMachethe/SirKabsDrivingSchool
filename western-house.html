Property Details

About Western House

Western House offers secure and affordable accommodation for students in Gqeberha. The property includes Wi-Fi, study areas, transport access, and modern rooms everything you need to focus on your studies.

  • Address: 15 Western Road, Port Elizabeth Central, Gqeberha, South Africa
  • Province: Eastern Cape
  • City: Gqeberha
  • Single Beds: 20 rooms
  • Sharing Beds: 74 beds (11 rooms)
  • Gender: Male & Female Students
  • NSFAS Accredited
  • Study Friendly Environment
